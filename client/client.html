About SchoolSync

Built for schools that care about clarity.

SchoolSync is a barcode-based attendance monitoring system designed to make daily check-ins fast, accurate, and visible to everyone who needs it.

Scan in seconds

Students scan their unique QR code at the door. No paper, no delays — every check-in is recorded instantly.

See the pattern

Teachers see their classes. Students see their own record. Admins see the whole picture in one dashboard.

Keep it accountable

Role-based access keeps private attendance details in the right hands — nothing more, nothing less.

How it works

Three steps. Zero confusion.

1

Register

Admin registers each student with their ID and department. A unique QR code is generated automatically.

2

Scan

Students scan their QR code using any camera. The system records the time and marks them Present or Late automatically.

3

Monitor

Teachers and admins review attendance in real time. Records are stored securely and can be filtered by date.

Who uses SchoolSync

A role for everyone in the school.

Admin

Manages students, teachers, and the full attendance log. Full access to all records and system settings.

Teacher

Views their class attendance, manages their schedule, and monitors student presence day by day.

Student

Scans their QR code to check in and views their personal attendance history and class schedule.

The system

Built on the web. Works everywhere.

SchoolSync runs entirely in the browser — no app install needed. It works on phones, tablets, and desktops. Powered by Node.js and MySQL, deployed on Railway.
