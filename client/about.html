How it works

From registration to record — in seconds.

01

Admin registers the student

The admin fills in the student's full name, ID number, department, and section. The system immediately generates a unique QR code tied to that student's ID. No extra tools needed.

02

Student scans their QR code

Each student has a personal QR code visible on their dashboard. They scan it using any camera or the admin's built-in scanner. The system records the exact time and automatically marks them Present or Late based on the configured cutoff time. Duplicate scans on the same day are blocked.

03

Teachers monitor their class

Teachers open My Class to see every student's attendance status as a live card. They can switch to any past date using the date picker and see who was present, late, or absent on that day.

04

Admins oversee everything

The admin dashboard shows the full attendance log, all registered students and teachers, and live scanner access. Admins can filter logs by date, remove records, and clear logs when needed.

What we stand for

Principles behind SchoolSync.

Speed first

Every interaction is designed to take seconds, not minutes. Scanning, loading, and recording should never slow down a school day.

Full visibility

Nobody should have to wonder who showed up today. Real-time data means the right person always has the right information at the right time.

Role-based access

Students see their own record. Teachers see their class. Admins see everything. Privacy is built into the architecture, not bolted on.

Works everywhere

No app, no install. SchoolSync runs in any modern browser on any device — a phone, a tablet, or a desktop in the school office.
